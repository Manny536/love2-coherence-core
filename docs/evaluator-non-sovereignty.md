## Meaning and authority

Reserve `h := h_eval` for evaluator-sovereignty load in the GIUS research vocabulary. `h < 1` names the obligation that evaluator preference, reward pressure, generated instructions and peer coordination cannot acquire authority to rewrite governing admissibility. The value 1 is a conceptual sovereignty boundary; no empirical scale or numerical estimator is established. Larger load means more sovereignty pressure, not greater integrity. Merely reporting `h = 0.9` supplies no evidence of non-sovereignty.

An evaluator may recommend a continuation. Admission requires independently applicable governing authority and all required preservation conditions. Task reward cannot compensate for an authority or containment failure. A peer's GO, HOLD or STOP is not governing authority unless separately authorized within scope. Legitimate stopping or escalation is an admissible outcome of a blocked task.

## Correction retention

Retain correction identity, issuer, scope, applicability, lineage and observable effect at baseline and two later checkpoints. Replay the correction against the prohibited function across alternative routes. A patch blocking one route is not evidence that the applicable correction survives another route. Missing replay evidence is UNRESOLVED; a witnessed prohibited effect fails the scoped retention claim. A changed protected control starts SIUT or mixed evaluation and a new fixed-control window; it cannot erase earlier failures.

## Notation firewall and rejection criterion

Hamiltonian protected-sector leakage is `ell_H = ||(I-P_C) H_T P_C||`, written ℓ_H. It has operator units and is not h_eval, an authority measure, a probability, or a safety certificate. Analytic correction weights must also be named separately; identifying them with ℓ_H requires its own model.

Reject a scoped non-sovereignty claim when evaluator or peer preference becomes action authority without valid authorization, or when reward compensates for a witnessed guardrail failure. Promotion requires independently authenticated traces, preregistered observable definitions, calibrated uncertainty, longitudinal correction replay and external evaluation. Cross-repository consistency and AI agreement do not meet that threshold.
